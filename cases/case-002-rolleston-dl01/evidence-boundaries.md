# Case 002 Evidence Boundaries

## Purpose

This note governs publication of completed swings within the longitudinal Rolleston DL01 case. It allows a useful professional record without turning the repository into a live roster, work pack, maintenance history, or site record.

## Publication rule

A completed swing may publish:

- the public site and asset labels;
- Martin's employer, role, shift dates, and shift pattern;
- personally recalled tasks expressed within the Trade Assistant role;
- generic control vocabulary such as pre-start, WAF, JSA, permit, SLAM, barricading, isolation awareness, lockout-tagout awareness, and fire watch;
- bounded professional learning and improvement hypotheses.

Publication still excludes:

- names or identifying details of other workers, supervisors, and crews;
- copies of rosters, permits, isolations, work orders, JSAs, drawings, photographs, or internal messages;
- permit numbers, isolation points, access details, dimensions, specifications, quantities, or engineering criteria;
- live work-front status or information from a current shift;
- claims about equipment condition, compliance, quality, completion, commissioning, or return to service.

## Evidence status is not authority

A highly confident memory can support a personal-experience statement. It cannot create trade, electrical, rigging, permit, isolation, engineering, inspection, or operational authority.

| Evidence status | Meaning | Public treatment |
|---|---|---|
| `roster_supported` | A private roster corroborates identity, role, employer, or dates | Publish the supported fact; never publish the roster |
| `directly_recalled` | Martin personally remembers the fact | Publish after scope and confidentiality review |
| `confirmed_activity` | The activity occurred but timing or detail is incomplete | Publish the core activity with limitations |
| `partially_recalled` | The central fact is remembered but terminology or scope is uncertain | Use descriptive language and preserve uncertainty |
| `reconstructed_sequence` | Daily allocation is rebuilt from confirmed work blocks | Publish as retrospective reconstruction, never as an official log |
| `contextual_inference` | The detail is plausible but not remembered | Exclude from factual claims |
| `unknown` | Available evidence cannot support the statement | Preserve as unknown |

## Role-language controls

Use:

- `supported`, `assisted`, `prepared`, `transported`, `cleaned`, `marked`, `positioned`, `maintained fire watch`, and `worked under direction`;
- `the assigned boilermaker performed` for welding, oxy-cutting, and gouging;
- `assigned riggers or spotters controlled the movement` for crane activity;
- `permit/isolation awareness` unless Martin personally held a defined responsibility.

Do not use:

- `wired`, `connected`, `energised`, or `tested` for electrical setup;
- `welded`, `gouged`, or `oxy-cut` as Martin's actions;
- `approved`, `authorized`, `verified safe`, `confirmed isolation`, `inspected`, or `signed off`;
- `completed the repair` where the supported fact is participation in a continuing workstream.

## Specific competency boundaries

- Martin transported and extended temporary leads but did not make electrical connections.
- Martin did not enter confined spaces because the required authorization was not held.
- Martin used an angle grinder only on limited occasions when permitted and directed.
- Martin maintained fire watch during hot work but did not perform welding, oxy-cutting, or gouging.
- Crane movements were controlled by assigned rigging or spotting personnel.
- A single limited spotting interaction with mobile equipment does not establish a formal lifting or spotting role.

## Documentary evidence

A private roster screenshot was reviewed to corroborate Martin's name, employer, role, crew allocation, and shift dates. It contains identifying information about other people and must not be committed, reproduced, linked, or quoted.

The roster does not verify the daily task narrative. Task details remain retrospective self-report.

## Technical terminology

Where formal component terminology is uncertain, the record uses bounded descriptions. For example, the machinery-house task is described as work around the cable-reel drum and welded plate segments; no exact component name or engineering function is asserted.

## Longitudinal rule

Only completed swings may be developed publicly. Future rosters or active-shift information remain private until the relevant swing has ended, the account has been sanitized, and the evidence boundary has been reviewed.

## Interpretation

The case demonstrates disciplined translation of field experience into structured evidence. It is not an employer record, maintenance report, competency assessment, audit, incident report, or site-approved system.
