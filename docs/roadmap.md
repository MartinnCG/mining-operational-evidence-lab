# Evidence-Driven Roadmap

The roadmap advances by demonstrated capability, not calendar pressure.

## M0 — Foundation

**Question:** What is this repository allowed to claim and publish?

Evidence:

- architecture and repository interfaces;
- confidentiality and claims boundary;
- field-learning protocol;
- contribution rules and PR checklist;
- recorded field-design questions.

Exit: foundation PR reviewed and merged.

## M1 — Shutdown handover contract

**Question:** What minimum state must a synthetic incoming role receive?

Evidence:

- canonical event and state schema;
- lifecycle and allowed transitions;
- vocabulary and invariants;
- at least five synthetic happy-path and failure-path examples;
- tests for required fields and forbidden authority claims.

Exit: contract versioned and examples validate reproducibly.

## M2 — Synthetic scenario generator

**Question:** Can realistic operational complexity be generated without real site data?

Evidence:

- deterministic generator with seed;
- multiple shifts, work fronts, interruptions, blockers, and ownership transfers;
- clearly synthetic identifiers and timestamps;
- scenario manifest and data-quality report;
- adversarial cases: stale evidence, conflicts, duplicates, gaps, and late arrivals.

Exit: a clean checkout reproduces the same dataset and report.

## M3 — Operational Evidence Envelope integration

**Question:** Can mining-domain records interoperate with the portfolio evidence contract?

Evidence:

- mapping to Operational Evidence Envelope v1;
- provenance and synthetic markers;
- validation against shared schema;
- compatibility tests and documented deviations.

Exit: both repositories agree on the interface through linked PRs or versioned fixtures.

## M4 — Governed decision brief

**Question:** Can AI summarize a handover without inventing certainty or authority?

Evidence:

- integration with a governed AI/ML lab;
- supported-claim tracing;
- refusal on missing or contradictory evidence;
- evaluation set and error analysis;
- human-review boundary.

Exit: every output claim links to evidence or is explicitly marked as unsupported.

## M5 — Longitudinal synthetic study

**Question:** What patterns emerge across multiple shutdown cycles?

Evidence:

- versioned synthetic cycles;
- metrics for evidence completeness, staleness, ownership clarity, and handover latency;
- documented findings and rejected hypotheses;
- portfolio case study suitable for interviews and client discovery.

Exit: reproducible study plus a plain-language architecture narrative.

## Future option: geospatial operations layer

Only after M3, evaluate a public/synthetic link to Corredor de Altura:

- work-front location abstractions;
- access or dependency segments;
- change control by segment;
- spatial provenance.

No hardware is required for this layer. Hardware becomes relevant only for a separately justified evidence-capture experiment.
